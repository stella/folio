Dragging a content control or table that holds a footnote, endnote or comment now moves it with its note or comment under the same id, and dragging an image or kept block markup of the document keeps its relationship instead of copying or refusing it. A copy that starts between a content control's paragraphs, or inside a table within a control, now pastes the paragraphs or the table cells it holds, and nested table captures are dropped from pasted tables.
