A manifest section says when it shows a header or footer of its own (`ownHeaders`): for the other pages or even pages, content other than the section before it shows there, such as an annex's header or a signature page's footer. A plan can keep such a section a section, so that content gets a place. The last section of an output whose plan ends sections now begins on a new page unless the plan says otherwise, as every other section the plan ends does.
