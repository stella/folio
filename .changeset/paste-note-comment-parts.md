A copied footnote, endnote or comment holding a chart, an embedded object or kept block markup now pastes with it, within one document or into another, directly or as a suggestion: the parts it names are copied in, and the save writes their relationships into the note or comment part's own relationships. A pasted note may now hold kept block markup beside its paragraphs. The package model gains `annotationRelationships`, the relationships of the footnotes, endnotes and comments parts, and the package resources operation carries changes to them.
