With `textColors: "keep"` a plan carries the text colours and run shading that mark part of the source's text (a red note, a grey placeholder); a colour that covers more than half of the source's text is its own look and stays the template's. A content control among runs (plain text, rich text, date, list) stays around its content where the output places all of it, without the data binding, placeholder and run look that name parts of the source. The report's `sourceHeaders.pageNumbersTwice` counts output headers and footers that show two page numbers, the template's and the source's.
