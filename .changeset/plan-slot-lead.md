A slot of a template plan can drop a lead of the source block it takes: the name of a field the template shows itself (`To:` in front of an addressee, where the template has a cell that says so). The plan names the kept template paragraph that shows the field; the value is placed word for word, verification holds it to the source without the lead, and the report names every lead dropped (`droppedLead` on the block's result).
