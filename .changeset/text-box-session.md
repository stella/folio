Edit text box paragraphs in the canonical session: typing, public operations, tracked and suggested edits, comments and revision review reach a text box's paragraphs, and save writes them into the box (and its alternate content fallback) instead of refusing. `locateParagraphThroughTextBoxes` and `paragraphsThroughTextBoxes` find a story's paragraphs together with those of its text boxes.
