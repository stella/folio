A template plan carries more of the source. An object's embedded part travels whole with it: an OLE object's storage and an embedded package (a workbook, a document, a presentation) go into the output's `embeddings` folder with their content types, beside the picture that previews them, in the body and in carried headers and footers. A moved object declares on itself every namespace prefix its source bound further up (a source may bind the drawing prefixes once at its root, which the template's root need not do), so it reads back as its source. A plan's paragraph indent may give `left` a negative value, reaching into the margin as the source's paragraph does; a hanging or first-line indent stays a width.
