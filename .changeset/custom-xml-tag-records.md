A suggested paste or move of a block custom XML element now records the insertion or deletion of the element's own tags on the element, written as the custom XML range markers around it, and documents carrying those markers open and save with them. The review panel lists the record, on the card of the content suggested with it, and accepting or rejecting the tags and their content in any order leaves the same document. A suggested paste of a paragraph whose copied deleted or moved-away break holds a cut of its own now records that cut beside the paste's insertion of the break, resolving as the direct paste does.
