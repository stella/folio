Comparison pairs two paragraphs by their paragraph id only when their wording agrees with it, by the measure it pairs other paragraphs with. A paragraph whose id now names other words, as the paragraph a join leaves can, is aligned by its content, so a moved paragraph reads as a move.

A block redline inserts a paragraph before a heading that states its outline level as the paragraph the revised version holds: it clears the outline level it does not state, as it clears the anchor's style, alignment, spacing and indentation.
