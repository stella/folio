Removing a pending change reads the kept markers beside it that name its side as their cut's piece, instead of its own kept date. A deferred seam's blockers name their side, or both sides, on the markers standing there, and version 1 files mark the markers a deferred seam's kept date names. A deferred seam no longer records a kept date or count, and placing kept markers once its blockers are accepted reads the markers; they stand where they are when the same resolution empties the piece across the blockers.
