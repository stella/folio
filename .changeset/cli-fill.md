`folio fill` (MCP `fill_template`) builds a document from a template and Markdown or block-list content in one direct write: it keeps the template's sections, headers, footers, front matter and an optional trailing block, styles the content through a map to the template's paragraph styles and numbering, and fills `{{name}}` fields and exact-text replacements in every story. String flags a command declares read `@file`.
