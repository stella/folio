`folio literal-numbering` (MCP `numbering`) writes list labels as literal text, displayed or counted continuously in a house-style format such as `[{n}.]`, and removes list membership while keeping the level's indentation, tab stops and label formatting; `folio apply-numbering` puts paragraphs back into a numbering instance, stripping a leading literal label.
