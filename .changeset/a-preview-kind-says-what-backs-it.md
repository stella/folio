Split the preview table by what backs the preview. A source-backed kind keeps the strings the budget matches a `src` by and the cap it charges against; a descriptor-backed kind declares neither, so the diagram entry no longer carries a data-URL prefix nothing starts with, a character cap over no characters, or the mime type and filename the retired raster was stamped with. Only a source-backed kind can be named in a budget override or returned by the matcher.
