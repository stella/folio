A template plan can give a paragraph a space of its own in front of it and behind it (`spacing` on a source item and on a nested paragraph), over what its style states: for a line the document sets tight or apart by itself, such as the rows of a table. The block manifest reports the space a paragraph states for itself (`spacing` on a block), and verification holds the output to the plan.
