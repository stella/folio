A numbering patch of a template plan can set the face of a level's label (`font`), and a block of the manifest names the text face a bullet's mark is a character of (`numbering.bulletFont`). A bullet written as a letter of a text face, the customary `o` of a monospace face, is that shape only in that face; a plan that moved such a bullet onto another list printed the letter in the paragraph's face.
