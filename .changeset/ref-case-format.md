A template plan's reference may ask for its result in a letter case: `\n \* lower \h`, `\* Upper`, `\* FirstCap`. The field is written with the switch, and the predicted result of a numbered field is the target's label in that case, a roman numeral included, as the reference consumer prints it. A citation that spells the label's words in another case ("art. 3" for a label `Art. 3`) can so be one field over word and number. Any other format switch is refused as before.
