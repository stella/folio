A kept range marker's cut names the side whose record is a piece of it (`piece`), set where a pending change takes the marker's seam; accepting that change reads the markers' cut instead of the change's own kept date. Review history version 2 carries it on the marker, and version 1 files mark the markers their changes name.

Kept markers a seam deferred on pending insertions go into no piece once those are rejected when the records the seam meets are not alike: they stand where they are, as rejecting the insertions first leaves them.
