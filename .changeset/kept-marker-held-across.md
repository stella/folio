Resolving tracked changes keeps a range marker kept from a rejected link at the seam, in every order, when a join is held on its other side: an earlier pending insertion's, a pending deletion's, or a seam either handed over. A marker whose piece a pending deletion holds follows that deletion's seam once it is accepted, an insertion emptied by accepting the deletion it held hands its join on as accepting it does, and a removed insertion's cut goes with an earlier insertion it was made in past later insertions the same rejection removes.
