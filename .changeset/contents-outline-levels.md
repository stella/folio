A source item may carry its own outline level (`outlineLevel`, 0 for level 1), written as the paragraph's `w:outlineLvl`, and `fields.contentsOutlineLevels` gives the template's kept contents fields the `\u` switch, so they also list paragraphs by their own outline level: a heading the source's contents listed that the template's field does not collect by style.
