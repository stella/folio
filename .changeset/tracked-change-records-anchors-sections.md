Deleting a paragraph whose hyperlink, content control or smart tag holds a comment anchor now works as a suggestion: the anchor stays where the content was, and rejecting restores the link whole. A pending change to the final section's properties can be accepted, rejected and re-attributed with the rest of a document's changes. A suggested paragraph that ends a section carries its break with its inserted mark, and a table of contents can be generated over headings and text that hold pending changes.
