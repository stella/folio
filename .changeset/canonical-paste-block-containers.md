Canonical sessions now paste and drag block content controls, custom XML elements and kept block markup whole, and a copy that starts or ends inside a container pastes the content it copied. Text copied from outside the editor with comment or note markers pastes without them, and copied footnotes and endnotes bring their images and links. Documents holding a table-review carrier paragraph now open and edit, and composing inside an inline content control commits as typing does. Refusals that describe a malformed document or request, or a session defect, now report a condition instead of a gap.
