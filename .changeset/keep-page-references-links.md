More of a source's fields and links survive a template plan. A reference may be written as a `PAGEREF` (`field: "PAGEREF"`, with `\\h`, `\\p`, a numeric picture and a look-keeping format), and the manifest lists PAGEREF fields among a paragraph's cross-references, including those inside a table of contents' result. `internalLinks: "keep"` links the source's links to its own bookmarks (`w:anchor`, `HYPERLINK \\l`) to the plan's bookmark on the output paragraph that stands for their target; those left as text are counted. A contents entry field (`TC`) and a macro button (`MACROBUTTON`) stay fields where their paragraph is placed.
