Italic that a text paragraph style gives its paragraphs travels onto the template: every run of such a paragraph states it, so a drafting note or a comment set apart by an italic body style stays italic in a template style that is upright. A run that turns the style's italic off stays upright. A heading's style (one with an outline level, or named as a heading, title or subtitle) keeps its italic to itself, as the template gives headings their look.
