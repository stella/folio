A manifest read with the tracked changes kept says where each paragraph's changes sit: `revisions` on a block lists insertions by the text they added and deletions by the offset they were made at, with author and date, and marks a change of the paragraph mark. A planner can then leave a label or a reference alone where a change touches it instead of learning it from a refusal.
