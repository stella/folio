Canonical rooms gain two transports and presence. `openCanonicalTabRoom` shares a room between the tabs of one origin: the tab holding the room's Web Lock runs the authority, every tab reaches it over a BroadcastChannel, and a tab taking over the lock rebuilds the sequencer from the durable journal (`openCanonicalIndexedDbJournal`) before it leads. `openCanonicalYjsRoom` carries a room in a Yjs document for hosts that sync one: clients append to an inbox, and the authority helper a host server runs (`serveCanonicalYjsRoom`) sequences the inbox, appends to the journal and answers each request; a client refuses a journal entry written by any other Yjs client. `restoreCanonicalRoomAuthority` rebuilds a room's sequencer from its stored records. With the `presence` option, `joinCanonicalRoom` shares each peer's selection in canonical positions over the transport's presence channel, moves the other peers' selections over every sequenced batch it applies, and gives the host each peer's selection in the session's projection. A lease call a room can no longer forward, because its transport closed or stopped following the journal, is answered with the new `unavailable` lease refusal (`ID_LEASE_REFUSAL_REASONS.UNAVAILABLE`), and a tab whose journal write fails stands for the lock again instead of waiting on a term nobody leads.
