The block manifest reports, for a paragraph numbered by a list level other than a bullet, the level's text (`lvlText`, such as `%1. %2)`), the format it counts in (`numFmt`) and whether it prints its counters in arabic numerals (`legal`, from `w:isLgl`). A plan's numbering patch may switch legal numbering on or off for a level (`isLgl`); verification holds the output to it.
