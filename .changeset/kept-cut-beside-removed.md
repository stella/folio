A kept range marker left with nothing on one side by a later resolution keeps one level of cut, and a marker between pieces of one rejected insertion keeps no deeper cut, as resolving them with the revision it was kept from does. A piece a kept marker names goes with the change that took its seam; a pending paragraph mark beside the marker takes none. A pending paragraph mark waits beside kept markers across range boundaries between the paragraphs and from inside a record at the paragraph's edge. A seam's join stays on the seam when the pending insertion facing it refuses it, and resolution no longer reads a list's last item as the one before its first.
